Support

Need help with Late Show? Email vikingershaker@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within two days.

Restore your Collector’s Edition

Open Settings → Restore Purchase, or tap Restore on the Collector’s Edition screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Watch parties

Everyone needs a free Late Show account. The host starts a party in the Party tab and shares the 6-letter code; friends enter it under Join a friend. Each person plays the movie in their own streaming app when the countdown ends.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and synced data.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
